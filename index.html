Privacy Commitment & Store Compliance Notice This Privacy Policy strictly adheres to the Google Play Store Developer Program Policies (User Data, Location, and Data Safety Policies) and Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1 - Privacy and Data Use). The One Indicator – Pune Public Transport app is designed as a privacy-friendly public transit utility. We do not require account registration, do not track you in the background, and never sell your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to One Indicator – Pune Public Transport ("One Indicator", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"). One Indicator is a dedicated public transit utility application engineered to assist commuters, residents, and visitors in the Pune Metropolitan region (Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad). The Application provides information regarding Pune's public transportation network, including:

  • PMPML (Pune Mahanagar Parivahan Mahamandal Ltd.) city bus routes, schedules, stops, and fare charts.
  • Pune Metro (Maha Metro) Purple Line, Aqua Line, and Hinjawadi Metro Line 3 station maps, timetable, and ticket fares.
  • Pune Suburban Local Trains (Pune Junction, Shivajinagar, Khadki, Dapodi, Kasarwadi, Pimpri, Chinchwad, Akurdi, Dehu Road, Talegaon, Lonavala) timetables and platforms.
  • Auto Rickshaw and Taxi tariff cards and fare calculators.
  • Emergency transport helpline numbers, transit announcements, and route planning tools.

We are committed to maintaining the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is processed locally on your device or via external services, the purpose of each permission requested, and your privacy rights under applicable data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) of India, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

3. Information We Collect

We believe in the principle of strict Data Minimization. We only access or collect information that is strictly required to provide transit navigation services.

A. Information You Are NOT Required to Provide

  • No Personal Identification: You do NOT need to provide your real name, email address, home address, or phone number to use One Indicator.
  • No Account Registration: The application functions completely without user registration, usernames, passwords, or social login authentication.
  • No Financial Information: We do not collect credit cards, debit cards, UPI credentials, or bank details. All timetable and route data is free to access.

B. Information Collected or Processed by the Application

  • Location Data (Optional, Foreground-Only): If you grant permission, your device's approximate or precise GPS coordinates are accessed exclusively while the app is active to show nearby bus stops, metro stations, and relevant route connections.
  • Transit Search Queries (Processed Locally): Station names, bus route numbers, or source/destination stops you search for are processed directly on your device.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information (Automated, Non-Identifiable): Standard non-identifiable technical data such as device model, OS version, app version, language preference, and anonymous crash telemetry (for debugging application crashes).

4. Location Information (Google Play & App Store Disclosures)

Location access is an optional feature designed solely to enhance your commuting experience across Pune.

Location Usage Summary

  • Purpose: To calculate distances to the nearest PMPML bus stops, display nearby Pune Metro stations, and sort transit options based on your current vicinity.
  • Type of Location: Approximate Location (network-based) or Precise Location (GPS-based), depending on the permission granted in your system dialog.
  • Foreground Only: Location data is accessed only while the application is open and visible on your screen (in the foreground).
  • NO Background Tracking: One Indicator never requests, collects, accesses, or monitors your location in the background or when the app is closed.
  • No Tracking History: We do not log, record, build profiles of your daily movements, or transmit your geographical coordinates to external marketing servers. Coordinates used for calculating distances to nearby stops are computed ephemerally in volatile memory or via secure map APIs.

How to Control or Disable Location Access

Location permission is completely optional. You can deny location access during the initial prompt or revoke it at any time:

  • On Android: Open Settings > Apps > One Indicator > Permissions > Location and select "Don't allow".
  • On iOS: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > One Indicator and choose "Never".

Notice: If you deny or revoke location access, all core features of One Indicator remain 100% functional. You can still manually search for any bus number, metro station, local train schedule, or route by typing the station or stop name.

5. Transport Search Information & Offline Storage

When you search for PMPML bus routes (e.g., Katraj to Swargate, Pune Station to Hinjawadi Phase 3), Pune Metro lines (Vanaz to Ramwadi, PCMC to Civil Court / Swargate), or Pune-Lonavala local train timetables:

  • Local Query Processing: Route calculations and schedule lookups are executed against local offline databases or cached datasets stored within your device's private app sandbox.
  • Recent Searches & Bookmarks: Your saved routes, favorite bus numbers, and recent search history are stored strictly locally on your device (using Android Room / SQLite / Shared Preferences / iOS UserDefaults).
  • No Personal Profiling: We do not associate your transport lookups with any personal identity, email, or advertising profile.

6. Device Information & Technical Diagnostics

When the application communicates with online services (such as downloading updated PMPML timetables or refreshing transit notices), standard HTTP technical information is exchanged:

  • Device Model & Manufacturer: E.g., Samsung Galaxy, Xiaomi, iPhone 14. Used to ensure correct UI rendering and layout scaling.
  • Operating System & Build Version: E.g., Android 14, iOS 17. Used to verify API compatibility.
  • Application Version: E.g., Version 1.0.0. Used to check for necessary schedule updates.
  • Crash Logs & Stack Traces: In the event of an unexpected crash or freeze, an anonymous diagnostic log (excluding personal files or identifying data) may be collected via Google Play Console or Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs and prevent future crashes.
  • IP Address: Received ephemerally by servers during network requests to deliver timetable files, as required by the standard TCP/IP networking protocol. IP addresses are not stored or used to track individual users.

12. Data Security & Encryption

We implement rigorous administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect all data processed by the application:

  • Encryption in Transit: All network communications with our servers, timetable feeds, and third-party APIs are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3). Plaintext (HTTP) traffic is strictly blocked.
  • Sandboxed Local Storage: User preferences, offline timetable caches, and bookmarks are stored within the secure internal application sandbox on Android and iOS, preventing other applications from accessing the files.
  • No Credential Vulnerabilities: Because the application does not utilize user passwords, login credentials, or financial payment details, there is zero risk of password credential leaks or credit card exposure from our app.

13. Data Retention & Deletion Mechanisms

Our retention policies adhere to the principle that data is kept only as long as necessary for its intended purpose:

  • Ephemeral Location Data: Location coordinates used to calculate nearby stops exist only in volatile system memory (RAM) during calculation and are discarded immediately afterward.
  • Offline Timetables & Search Cache: Stored on your device until refreshed with new schedule updates or until you clear application storage.
  • Diagnostic & Crash Reports: Retained anonymously on Google Cloud / Firebase Crashlytics servers for a maximum of 90 days before automated deletion.
  • Immediate Local Deletion: You can purge all cached data and stored preferences at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.
