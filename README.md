## 🚀 How to Enable GitHub Pages (1-Minute Setup)

1. Go to your GitHub repository: [https://github.com/wittyrusher/oneindicatorprivacy](https://github.com/wittyrusher/oneindicatorprivacy)
2. Click **Settings** (top right tab of the repo).
3. In the left sidebar, click **Pages** (under *Code and automation*).
4. Under **Build and deployment** &gt; **Source**, select **"Deploy from a branch"**.
5. Under **Branch**, select `main` and folder `/(root)`.
6. Click **Save**.
7. In about 30 seconds, your privacy policy webpage will be live at:
   `https://wittyrusher.github.io/oneindicatorprivacy/`

## 📱 Google Play Console Submission Guide

To resolve Google Play Store rejection and achieve instant approval:

### 1. Update the Privacy Policy URL in Play Console:
1. Open the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. Select **One Indicator**.
3. In the left navigation, scroll to **Policy and programs** &gt; **App content**.
4. Click **Start** or **Manage** next to **Privacy policy**.
5. Paste your live URL:
   ```text
   https://wittyrusher.github.io/oneindicatorprivacy/
   ```
6. Click **Save**.

### 2. Data Safety Declaration (Google Play):
Under **App content** &gt; **Data safety**:
- **Location**:
  - Approximate Location &amp; Precise Location: **Yes (Collected)**
  - Is it processed ephemerally? **Yes**
  - Is this data required or optional? **Users can choose whether this data is collected (Optional)**
  - Why is it collected? **App functionality** (To discover nearby PMPML bus stops and Pune Metro stations)
  - Is it shared with third parties? **No**
- **App info and performance**:
  - Crash logs &amp; Diagnostics: **Yes** (Collected anonymously for app stability via Firebase Crashlytics)
  - Shared: **No**
- **Personal Information**:
  - Name, Email, Phone, User IDs: **No data collected**
- **Account Deletion Declaration**:
  - Does your app allow users to create an account? **No** (The app does not support or require user registration/accounts).

## 🍏 Apple App Store Connect Guide

1. Log in to [Apple App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com).
2. Go to **My Apps** &gt; **One Indicator** &gt; **App Information**.
3. Under **Privacy Policy URL**, paste:
   ```text
   https://wittyrusher.github.io/oneindicatorprivacy/
   ```
4. Under **App Privacy**:
   - **Data Used to Track You**: None
   - **Data Linked to You**: None
   - **Data Not Linked to You**:
     - *Location* (Coarse &amp; Precise) &rarr; App Functionality
     - *Diagnostics* (Crash Data) &rarr; Analytics / App Functionality
